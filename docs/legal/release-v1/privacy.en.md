Darb Privacy Policy

Version 1 - Last updated: [DATE TO BE COMPLETED AT PUBLICATION]

This policy explains what personal data Darb collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do. The controller of your data is [LEGAL ENTITY NAME - TO BE COMPLETED] ("Darb", "we"). Contact: darb@aynur.ps.

1. Data we collect
Account data
- Your name, your email address, a contact mobile number, your role (passenger, driver or merchant) and your city if you give it.
- The contact mobile number is the number you type when you sign up. We do not verify it and we store it as you entered it.
- A password, stored only as a one-way hash (we cannot read your password). If you sign in with Google, we store the identifier Google gives us.
- Records that you accepted the Terms, the Privacy Policy and the 18+ declaration, with the version and the time.

Trips, requests and orders
- Your ride requests, bookings, parcel orders and their status and times, the number of seats, and the pick-up and drop-off places. Pick-up and drop-off are the text you type or the place you pick, and may include an address or a landmark.
- For parcels: the receiver's name and phone number and an optional note, which the sender provides, and a hand-over code (stored only in a protected form).
- Cancellation reasons.

Location
- For drivers: the live location of the device while a trip is active, so the passenger and Darb can follow the trip. We do not record your location when you have no active trip.
- Places or coordinates you choose in the app, and the location you attach to a community report.

Driver and merchant documents
- Photos or files of documents such as a driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and an identity photo, with the review notes and decisions. They are stored in Darb's database.

Assistant
- The messages you write to the Ask Darb assistant and its replies (your chat history).

Ratings and reports
- Ratings and comments about trips and drivers, ratings of the app, incident reports (category, description and the trip concerned) and community reports about roads or checkpoints (category, description, place) with votes and flags on them.

Notifications
- If push notifications are enabled and you allow them: a device push token, the platform (Android or iOS), the app version and your language. You can turn them off in your device settings or in the app.

Technical data
- Sign-in session information (device name, time of use), IP address, request logs, error logs and security logs, which we use to keep the Service secure and working.

2. Why we use it
- To create and secure your account and to sign you in.
- To match passengers, drivers and parcels, to run trips and to show you their status.
- To review driver and merchant applications and to keep the Service safe.
- To show live trip information and to send you notifications about your trips.
- To handle ratings, incident reports, community reports and support.
- To prevent fraud and misuse and to find and fix technical problems.
- To suggest trips through the assistant.
- To meet our legal duties and to establish or defend legal claims.

3. Who sees what
- Passengers: after a driver accepts your request, you see the driver's name, vehicle, plate number and phone number. Before acceptance you do not.
- Drivers: after accepting, a driver sees the details of the request needed to carry it out, such as the passenger's name, the pick-up and drop-off, the number of seats or the parcel. Drivers do not see more than the trip needs.
- Receivers: a parcel receiver is contacted using the details the sender gave, and receives a hand-over code.
- Community: community reports (category, description and place) are visible to other signed-in riders and drivers.
- Darb administrators see account, trip, document and report data when needed to review applications, to support users and to deal with incidents. Access is limited to administrators and recorded in an audit log.
- Service providers working for us under our instructions: our hosting provider, Injazat; an email sending service for messages to you; map and place services; a push notification service if push is enabled; and the artificial intelligence provider that processes your assistant messages (see section 4). We give them only what is needed for their task.
- Authorities, if the law or a valid legal order requires it.
We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. The assistant
The text you type to Ask Darb is sent to an external artificial intelligence provider so that it can understand your request and suggest a trip. Do not type sensitive information such as identity numbers, passwords or health details. The chat history is stored in Darb's database so you can continue the conversation.

5. How long we keep data
- Driver and merchant documents: deleted automatically 90 days after the review decision is made, unless we must keep them longer to resolve a dispute, or the law requires it.
- Parcel receiver details (name, phone number and note): erased after the delivery period ends; our current setting is 14 days after the end of the requested delivery window.
- Sign-up attempts, one-time codes, security counters and expired sessions: kept for a short time and removed automatically.
- Push tokens: pushes stop when the sign-in session ends, and the token is deleted when you delete your account.
- Records of your acceptance of these documents: kept for as long as your account exists and for the period the law requires after that.
- Trip, order, rating, incident and report records: kept for as long as needed for safety, support, accounting and disputes. After that they are deleted or made anonymous. [RETENTION PERIOD TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE OWNER'S LAWYER]
- Logs: kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. [LOG RETENTION PERIOD TO BE CONFIRMED BY THE OWNER]
- Backups: deleted data may remain in encrypted backups until they are overwritten in the normal cycle.

6. Your rights
You can ask us to: tell you what data we hold about you; correct data that is wrong; delete your account and data; stop a particular use; or give you a copy. We may keep information that the law requires us to keep or that we need to protect others, and we will tell you if so. To make a request, write to darb@aynur.ps from the email address of your account. We will answer within a reasonable time. You can also turn off location and push notifications in your device settings, but some features will then stop working.

7. How we protect data
- Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Passwords and hand-over codes are stored only as hashes.
- Access for administrators is restricted by role and recorded.
- Drivers' and users' private details are shown to others only when a trip needs it.
- Sign-in sessions can be revoked and expire.
No system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us at once if you think someone else has your account.
